## Program & Product Operations | AI Enablement | Customer Experience

I like figuring out how things work, then figuring out how to make them work better.

At Microsoft, that meant writing technical guidance, learning Power Automate to fix a frustrating intake process, helping product teams become confident contributors, and building repeatable ways to keep documentation current and useful.

This portfolio shows both the customer-facing work and the systems behind it: what I built, how I approached the problems, and what I learned along the way.
