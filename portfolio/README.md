## Selected work

### Turning scattered requests into a system of work

One of my favorite projects started with requests arriving through Teams, email, meetings, and GitHub—or never getting submitted at all.

I taught myself Power Automate and built a structured intake process connecting Microsoft Forms, Azure DevOps, acknowledgment emails, and reporting. I also linked documentation changes to their original requests and review context. The approach continued across teams and changes in ownership, and I later began exploring an agentic intake experience in Copilot Studio.

[Read the automated intake case study](case-studies/automated-intake.md)

### Building a repeatable approach to freshness and AI readiness

Keeping hundreds of articles current takes more than a list of overdue reviews.

Starting in my first months at Microsoft, I built and refined freshness processes across Partner Center, Marketplace, Microsoft Sales Experience, and Power BI/Fabric. I combined technical ownership, customer usage, prioritization, and regular follow-through, then extended that work to content structure, metadata, and AI readiness.

[Read the freshness and AI-readiness case study](case-studies/freshness-ai-readiness.md)

### Helping product teams contribute independently

I created contributor guidance, provided walkthroughs and hands-on support, and presented in contributor lunch-and-learn sessions. That work helped product managers become more comfortable managing their own documentation changes and supported transitions to self-service.

[Read the contributor enablement case study](case-studies/self-service-contribution.md)

### Guiding customers from Power BI Premium to Microsoft Fabric

I authored four connected Microsoft Learn articles covering the migration overview, capacity decisions, procedures, and common questions. As the project's knowledge engineer, I drafted the guidance and managed successive rounds of technical review with SMEs across teams.

The case study also covers a memorable AI-assisted authoring error: an internal planning target became a false customer deadline as context was lost between handoffs.

[Read the migration case study](case-studies/premium-to-fabric-migration.md) · [Explore published work](selected-work/selected-work.md)

### Reworking Marketplace guidance around the customer journey

I partnered with marketing and product engineering on a 3-month documentation revamp. The work combined article revisions, scenario-based navigation, a redesigned entry experience, and practical guidance that helped stakeholders move the project forward.

[Read the Marketplace case study](case-studies/marketplace-customer-revamp.md)

### Reorganizing Power BI navigation around scenarios and roles

I contributed to improvements across the Power BI documentation hub, landing pages, and tables of contents. Before-and-after examples show how the experience evolved to separate customer scenarios, audience roles, and tools more clearly.

[Read the Power BI navigation case study](case-studies/power-bi-navigation-revamp.md)

### Evaluating AI-assisted workflows

I co-led content-focused user acceptance testing for an AI-assisted
pull-request review workflow, organized scenarios and work-item tracking,
piloted AI-assisted review and content-quality tools, and helped turn
evaluation findings into reusable workflows.

[Read the AI workflow evaluation case study](case-studies/ai-workflow-evaluation.md)
